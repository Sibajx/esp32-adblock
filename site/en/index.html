Fewer ads on every device in your home.

SiBlock is a small board that joins your WiFi and keeps ads and trackers out. It works for your phone, your TV and your computer at the same time, without installing anything on each one. You set it up yourself from this page in a few minutes.

See what I need I already have the board

What it removes

  • Ads on web pages and in many apps.
  • Trackers that follow what you do.
  • With the extra list (off by default): scams and fake shops.

What it can’t remove

Ads on YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. They come from the same place as the video or the photos, so removing them would also remove what you want to see.

Why not (in Spanish)

  1. 1 Get what you need

    You can find everything in electronics stores or online shops.

    A classic ESP32 board
    Look for “ESP32 WROOM-32” with 4 MB and a USB port. It’s often called “ESP32 DevKit”. These won’t work: ESP8266 boards (for example, the D1 mini), other ESP32 models (S2, S3, C3, C6) or boards without a USB port.
    A USB data cable
    Many cables only charge, and the computer can’t see the board. If it doesn’t show up, try another cable first: it’s the most common problem.
    A 5 V charger
    Any phone charger will do to keep it on all day.
    2.4 GHz WiFi at home
    The board can’t join 5 GHz networks. Almost every modem has both; sometimes the 2.4 GHz one shows up as another network with a similar name.
    If the computer doesn’t recognize the board

    The board has a chip to talk over USB. It’s almost always CH340 or CP2102; you can read it on the small chip next to the connector.

    • Windows: it often installs by itself. If not, download the program from the chip maker: CH340 for Windows or CP2102 not checked yet.
    • Mac: if it doesn’t show up, install the maker’s one: CH340 for Mac or CP2102 not checked yet.
    • Linux and Chromebook: nothing to install.

  2. 2 Install SiBlock

    Plug the board into the computer with the cable and press the button.

    To install, use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari can’t do it yet.

    This page has to be opened with an address that starts with https.

    What to press in the installer window
    1. Pick your board in the list and press Connect.
    2. Press Install SiBlock. If it was already installed, the button says Update SiBlock.
    3. The first time it asks about Erase device: tick it and press Next. When updating it almost never shows up; if it does, leave it unticked so your WiFi isn’t erased.
    4. Confirm with Install and wait a few minutes without unplugging.
    5. In Connect to Wi-Fi pick your network, type its password in Password and press Connect.

    Button names when updating: not checked yet with the board.

    Your board is not in the list
    • Try another cable: many USB cables only charge and carry no data.
    • If the list is empty, close it and press the button again: the second time every port is shown.
    • On Linux, if Chromium or Brave came from the Ubuntu store (snap), it has no USB permission. Open a terminal, run sudo snap connect brave:raw-usb (or chromium:raw-usb), close the browser completely and open it again.
    • On Linux your user must also be in the dialout group.

    To install, use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Firefox and Safari can’t do it yet.

  3. 3 After installing

    • Your WiFi. The installer asks for it when it finishes. If you skipped it, press “Install SiBlock” again: it lets you enter it without installing again.
    • Save the backup password. It’s the password of SiBlock’s own WiFi. You need it if you ever change your WiFi.
    • Open your panel and create your password. It must be 12 to 64 characters long. If it asks you to press the BOOT button, press the board’s BOOT button once and try again. The panel on the board is only in Spanish for now.

    Your backup password and your panel

    Keep the board plugged in by USB and close the installer window. Then press the button and pick your board in the list.

    Once it gets your WiFi, SiBlock needs a couple of minutes to get ready. It won’t answer in the meantime: wait with the page open and don’t unplug the board.

    Open my panel

  4. 4 Tell your router to use SiBlock

    This way every device at home goes through SiBlock without touching them one by one. Before changing anything, take a photo of how it was: if something goes wrong, you can put it back.

    1. Open your router’s page. The address, user and password are usually on a label under the device.
    2. Find the list of connected devices and ask for SiBlock to always get the same address. It’s usually called “reserve IP” or “static IP”.
    3. Find the “DNS” part (sometimes inside “LAN” or “DHCP”). In the first DNS, type SiBlock’s address (you see it in step 3 or in your panel). Leave the second DNS empty (if it makes you fill it, use the same address): if you put another one, some devices use it and ads sneak in.
    4. If you see IPv6 options, turn off DNS over IPv6. If not, some devices skip SiBlock.
    5. Save, then turn your devices’ WiFi off and on so they pick up the change.

    Every router names it differently, and some internet companies don’t let you change the DNS.

What it can do

You control everything from your panel, on your phone or computer (the panel is in Spanish for now).

  • Remove ads and trackers on every device at home at once.
  • Name each device (“Living room TV”, “Ana’s phone”) and pause blocking on just one, for example if a page won’t load on the TV.
  • Schedules for the kids: cut off social media, games or the whole internet on the devices and at the times you choose.
  • Extra lists against scams and fake shops, and against some ads in Spanish (only a few for now). They're off by default; turn them on if you want.
  • Backup: save your settings to a file and move them to another board if you ever change it.

What does the light mean?

Many boards have a blue light next to the USB port. It tells you how SiBlock is doing without opening anything. Some boards don’t have that light; if yours doesn’t, use the panel to see how it’s doing (still to be checked which models have it).

  • Steady light All good: SiBlock is removing ads.
  • Slow blink It’s downloading the ad list. Wait a few minutes.
  • Fast blink Something is wrong: it can’t find your WiFi, it’s waiting for you to set it up, or it can’t reach the internet. Open your panel. If it doesn’t open, look on your phone for the network SiBlock-XXXX (the X’s change on each board), join it with the backup password and follow the steps.

If your board has the blue light and it’s off even though the board has power, unplug it and plug it back in. While you press the BOOT button, the light does other things: that’s normal. Many boards also have a red light; that one only means it has power.

If something gets stuck

The BOOT button on the board (the one to the right of the USB in the drawing above) gets you out of trouble. Hold it down as long as the table says, then let go.

How long you hold itWhat happens
A quick pressFor 30 minutes you can create the panel password.
3 to 8 seconds (let go when the blue light stays on)SiBlock opens its own network SiBlock-XXXX so you can give it another WiFi.
10 seconds or moreErases the settings: the WiFi, the panel password, device names, schedules, the charts, the extra lists and the sharing and Private Relay options (they go back to off). Your rules stay.

I forgot the panel password

Hold BOOT for 10 seconds or more. Then give it your WiFi again (with the installer on this page or through its own network) and create a new password.

I changed my WiFi

  1. Hold BOOT for 3 to 8 seconds. If you do nothing, SiBlock also opens its network on its own after 10 minutes without reaching your WiFi.
  2. On your phone, join the network SiBlock-XXXX with the backup password.
  3. A page opens: choose your new WiFi, type its password and tap “Conectar” (Connect).

If you lost the backup password, plug the board in by USB and press “Install SiBlock” again: it lets you give it the new WiFi without installing again.
